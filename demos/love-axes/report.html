Love Axes

Intimacy × Passion — the two orthogonal components of love.

Chart Brief

  • Big Idea: Intimacy and passion are the only truly orthogonal components of love — commitment correlates too tightly with intimacy to add a distinct axis — so a defensible model of love is a 2D map, and the four quadrants correspond to real, distinct relationship types.
  • Story arc: Relationship (with quadrant annotation)
  • Audience: Read (30s) / General / Persuasive
  • Style stance: INNOVATIVE — the whole Big Idea is the 2D structure. A traditional scatter without quadrant partitions + archetype labels would land as "some dots on a plane" and lose the argument. The composition is earned by the audience having 30 seconds to look and by the argument being irreducibly geometric.
  • chartType: layered scatter_plot — shaded quadrant backgrounds + midpoint reference rules + archetype points + archetype labels + faint quadrant-region labels
  • Alternates considered: (a) Sternberg triangle — chosen against because the Big Idea refutes the third axis; using the standard viz would visually contradict the argument. (b) 2×2 BCG-style matrix — cleaner but forces binary high/low, loses the "moderate-to-high passion" nuance.

Archetype coordinates are illustrative — plot any real relationship on the same axes and read its quadrant.
